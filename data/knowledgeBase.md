## Who is Gaza Gate?

Gaza Gate is a local online marketplace in Gaza that connects customers with local sellers and stores. You can browse products, order directly from sellers, track your orders, message sellers, and pay with cash on delivery within Gaza.

## How do I create a customer account?

Click Register on the customer login page, enter your name, email, and password, then verify your email with the code sent to your inbox. After verification, you can log in and start shopping.

## Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. You can register or log in using your Google account from the customer login page. If you already have an account with the same email, use the matching login method for that account.

## Why do I need to verify my email?

Email verification protects your account and ensures you can recover your password and receive important order updates. You must verify your email before you can fully use your account.

## How do I browse products?

Open the home page to see product categories and featured products. You can browse by category, open a product page for details and photos, or visit a seller's store to see all their available items.

## How do I use the shopping cart?

Add products to your cart from the product page, then open your cart to review items, change quantities, or remove products. When you are ready, proceed to checkout from the cart.

## What is the wishlist?

The wishlist lets you save products you like for later. Add items from the product page and view them anytime in your wishlist without placing an order yet.

## How do I place an order?

Browse products, add items to your cart, then proceed to checkout. Enter or select your delivery address (neighborhood, street, and notes), review your order, and submit it. The seller will review and accept or respond to your order.

## Can I order from more than one seller at once?

Yes. Your cart can include products from different sellers. At checkout, the platform creates a separate order for each seller so every store manages its own items and delivery.

## What payment methods are accepted?

Currently we support cash on delivery (COD) only. You pay in cash when your order is delivered to your address.

## What are the delivery areas?

Delivery is available within Gaza. During checkout, provide your neighborhood, street, and any delivery notes to help the seller reach you.

## How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on the seller, product type, and your location in Gaza. After the seller accepts your order, they will prepare it and update the status until it is ready and completed. Contact the seller through messages if you need an update.

## How can I track my order?

Go to My Orders in your account to see your orders and status updates. Statuses include pending review, accepted, in production, ready, completed, rejected, or cancelled.

## What do the order statuses mean?

- **Pending review:** the seller has received your order and has not accepted it yet.
- **Accepted:** the seller confirmed your order and will start preparing it.
- **In production:** the seller is preparing your order.
- **Ready:** your order is ready for delivery.
- **Completed:** the order was delivered successfully.
- **Rejected:** the seller could not fulfill the order.
- **Cancelled:** the order was cancelled.

## Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel an order from My Orders if it has not yet been completed or already cancelled. Open the order and use the cancel option. If the order is already being prepared or delivered, contact the seller through messages.

## What if the seller rejects my order?

If a seller rejects your order, the order status will show as rejected. You will not be charged since payment is cash on delivery. You can message the seller for clarification or order from another store.

## How do I add or update my delivery address?

Go to your profile and manage your saved addresses. You can add a new address with neighborhood, street, and notes, or update or delete an existing one. Saved addresses can be selected quickly during checkout.

## How do I update my profile?

Open your profile to update your name, avatar, and account details. Keeping your information current helps sellers and support reach you when needed.

## How do I contact a seller?

Open a product page or seller store and use the message option to start a conversation with the seller directly. You can ask about products, delivery, or your order.

## How do I leave a product review?

After receiving a product from an order, you can leave one review per product from your order details. Submit a rating from 1 to 5, an optional comment, and an optional photo. Reviews help other customers and sellers.

## When can I leave a review?

You can review a product from an eligible order. Completed or rejected orders can be reviewed right away. For other statuses, the order must remain in that status for at least 5 days before a review is allowed.


## Can the seller reply to my review?

Yes. Sellers can post one public reply to your review. You can see the seller's response on the product or store page.

## How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click Forgot password, enter your email, verify the code sent to you, then set a new password.

## I did not receive the verification or reset code. What should I do?

Check your spam folder and confirm you entered the correct email. You can request a new verification or reset code from the login page. If the problem continues, contact support at gazagate.support@gmail.com.

## How do notifications work?

You receive in-app notifications for important events such as order updates, messages, and review replies. Open Notifications in your account to read them, mark them as read, or delete them.

## How do I contact Gaza Gate support?

If the chatbot cannot answer your question, email our support team at gazagate.support@gmail.com with your name, account email, and a clear description of your issue.

## Can I become a seller on Gaza Gate?

Gaza Gate supports both customers and sellers. If you want to sell on the platform, register as a seller or switch to a seller account from the appropriate registration or account options. Seller accounts have a separate login and dashboard for managing products and orders.

## Is my personal information safe?

Gaza Gate uses secure authentication and account verification to protect your data. Never share your password with anyone. Contact support immediately if you notice suspicious activity on your account.

## What types of products can I find on Gaza Gate? 

Gaza Gate hosts local sellers offering a variety of products such as food, clothing, handmade goods, and other items available from stores in Gaza. Available products depend on what each seller lists on the platform.

## What should I do if a product is out of stock?

If a product shows limited stock or is unavailable, you may not be able to order the requested quantity. Check the product page or message the seller to ask about availability or restock timing.
