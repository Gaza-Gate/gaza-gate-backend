## Who is Gaza Gate?

Gaza Gate is a local online marketplace in Gaza that connects customers with local sellers and stores. You can browse products, order directly from sellers, track your orders, and message sellers, with cash-on-delivery payment within Gaza.

## How do I place an order?

Browse products, add items to your cart, then proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery address (neighborhood and street) and submit your order. The seller will review and accept it.

## What payment methods are accepted?

Currently we support cash on delivery (COD). Pay when your order is delivered to your address.

## How can I track my order?

Go to My Orders in your account to see order status updates such as pending review, accepted, in production, ready, and completed.

## How do I contact a seller?

Open a product or store page and use the message option to start a conversation with the seller directly.

## How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click "Forgot password", enter your email, verify the code sent to you, then set a new password.

## What are the delivery areas?

Delivery is available within Gaza. During checkout, provide your neighborhood, street, and any delivery notes to help the seller reach you.
